Bas ishaara kaafi hai.

Artha means meaning: अर्थ in Hindi and Marathi, అర్థం in Telugu, அர்த்தம் in Tamil.

Indians run their lives on short voice notes: “Anna ko bol do”, “Mehta ji wala appointment parso shift karwa do”. Speech models hear those words but miss the meaning. Artha uses what your phone already knows about your life to work out who and what you mean, and gets it ready to do in one tap.

01 · THE PROBLEM

India speaks in hints. Base models need instructions.

A voice note is six seconds long, mixes two languages, and leaves most things unsaid. Here is one real recording, run through the same speech model with and without Artha.

“Over a billion Indians have never typed a search into Gemini or ChatGPT intentionally.”

Keshav Reddy, founder of Equal AI. For them, voice is the way in. So Artha listens in their language, and answers out loud in their language too, so a user never has to read a screen or type a word to get something done.

Context→Intent→Action→Fulfilment→deeper context

This is Equal’s own loop (slide 5 of the kickoff deck), and “voice notes to actions” is Equal’s next product, after AI calls. Artha is the missing middle: turning a few spoken words plus personal context into the right intent.

04 · HOW IT WORKS

AI where it helps. Code where it must be exact.

Six stages. Each one is a separate module with its own tests, so the Equal team can read exactly what happens to a note.

A personal knowledge base, built for retrieval

People are tagged by what they are to you. Facts are linked to the person they’re about. Your corrections become rules. For each note, code pulls only what’s relevant, so the AI chooses from a short, explained list and never from the whole address book.

Fewer questions

Asking is a tax on the user. Artha acts on its best guess and makes changing it one tap. That tap is remembered.

ClearDo it. One tap to send.
CloseDo the best guess, with a small ⇄ for the runner-up. Picking it teaches Artha for next time.
True tieAsk once, with two big buttons. Only when scores are nearly equal.
MoneyNever guesses. Payments always wait for your confirmation.
OTPBlocked in code before any AI sees it.

In testing, Artha asked a question on — of notes.

06 · PROOF

Same recordings. Three systems. Answers written first.

Every clip has its correct answer written down before it runs. Each system hears the same audio and starts from a fresh profile. Base + pasted context is the obvious shortcut: the whole profile pasted into the prompt.

07 · THE FINALE

Three minutes on stage.

Lead with a recording the jury can feel, prove it with numbers, end on Equal’s roadmap. Every beat has a fallback.

Built this weekend

  • Transcription with name hints, script → Roman conversion
  • Indian dates, times and amounts in 4 languages, with tests
  • Knowledge base: tags, linked facts, corrections
  • Rules: OTP block, money confirmation
  • The app (React, Three.js, GSAP) and the eval

Reused

  • OpenAI: gpt-transcribe, gpt-6-luna, gpt-4o-mini-tts for test voices
  • Open source: React, Vite, Three.js, GSAP
  • AWS for hosting

Simulated, honestly

  • The four people and their histories are made up
  • Messages, calls and payments happen inside the app only
  • Test clips are synthetic voices, written in each language’s own script so names are said natively. Transcribing them back gives a 2% error, down from 7.8%. Real recordings are being added
