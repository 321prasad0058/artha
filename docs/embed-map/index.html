Same name, different people

Every person here has two people who share a name. In the embedding space they sit apart, because what Artha knows about each one is different. The number is cosine similarity between their two profiles (1 would mean identical). Tap a pair to see it on the map.

How this space is built

Each piece of text in a person's context space is turned into a vector of 256 numbers by the same model. Text that means similar things gets similar numbers, in any language. The map squeezes those 256 dimensions into the 2 that spread the points out most (principal component analysis), then nudges people apart just enough to keep every name readable. Treat distances as a faithful sketch, not exact.
